Stories

Stories are the main narrative surface of the universe. This paragraph belongs to the H2 section and stays visible when the H3 sections are folded.

The stories written in this universe

Every story in this universe starts from a single question, then follows the consequences until they become a place, a person, or a rumor.

Characters

Characters are the people who make the universe legible. The base version stays deliberately small so that every new character has room to grow.

The base version of characters in this universe

Each character has a public role, a private motive, and one detail that does not fit either. That third detail is usually where the story begins.

Universe

The universe is the set of locations, rules, and habits that make the stories feel like they share a home.

The basic version of locations in this universe

Locations are written as a small set of stable places, with enough friction between them to generate travel, trade, and misunderstanding.
